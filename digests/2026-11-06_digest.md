for us, on which target), `**Portability to vLLM (ROCm CDNA3/4):**` (the lift path on

## Signals across repos

### ROCm

- **Silent wrong output is still the dominant ROCm bug class:** aiter[#7258](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7258) (QuickReduce clip), aiter[#7182](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7182) (MLA splits stage 1 never wrote), aiter[#7092](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7092) (GSM8K 0.06 → 0.97), aiter[#7047](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7047) (NaN on ragged-K), sglang[#44268](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/44268) (GSM8K 0%), sglang[#45486](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45486) (1,024 → 896 reduction elements).
- **Determinism was broken, not merely slow:** sglang[#43788](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43788) made gpt-oss-20b and -120b give 50 and 47 different outputs for 50 identical requests on gfx950, and sglang[#44489](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/44489) then recovered 1.27–1.91× deterministic throughput by allowing a fixed-partition AITER decode.
- **Compiler and pin regressions moved the floor:** aiter[#7103](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7103) for ROCm 10.1, vllm[#65937](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65937) moving the AITER pin to v0.1.28.post1, and vllm[#61866](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/61866) closed once v0.1.26 carried aiter[#6336](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/6336).
- **Big landed e2e wins on gfx950:** vllm[#64763](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64763) (+18.7% at C8 by dropping torch.compile), vllm[#63440](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63440) (+57% interactivity for Muse Spark), sglang[#48490](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48490) (+12.5% at c32, off main).

### Quantization

- **MXFP4 is where the format bugs live:** aiter[#7317](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7317) (22.9 → 7.84 µs narrow-N), aiter[#6734](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/6734) (Hadamard fused into the quant), aiter[#7554](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7554) (K-tail mask 127 → 0), aiter[#7230](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7230) (missing TP8 configs, 5–20% regression).
- **FP8 KV keeps producing accuracy cliffs:** vllm[#64106](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/64106) (GSM8K 0.975 → 0.305), sglang[#48434](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48434) (0.000 vs 0.948 on a calibrated `k_scale`), sglang[#48500](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48500) (scale floor 2^-13 → 2^-22).
- **Quant plumbing reached main:** vllm[#62750](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62750) (block Hadamard input rotation, online `mxfp4_hadamard32`), vllm[#64004](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64004) (gfx942 MXFP4→int4 requant 19 min → 25 s).

### Online quantization

- **Rotation is being folded into the kernel that quantizes:** aiter[#6734](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/6734) rotates in registers inside the per-1x32 MXFP4 quant and the AR + RMSNorm epilogue (gfx950 only); vllm[#62750](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62750) is the PyTorch-side wrapper, with vllm[#63272](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63272) as the follow-up that removes the serving cost.
- **Quark online-rotation checkpoints are loadable now:** vllm[#62750](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62750) drops the int8 `input_rotation` after checking it, which is what an `amd/*-Quark-MXFP4` checkpoint carries.
- **Per-token FP8 quant is moving into norms:** atom[#2739](https://github.com/ROCm/ATOM/pull/2739) folds it into GemmaRMSNorm (4.12 µs per GDN layer, ~0.25 ms per decode iteration), and atom[#2980](https://github.com/ROCm/ATOM/pull/2980) reads FP8 planes in place for mono decode.

### Elsewhere

- **Architecture-neutral findings that bit us anyway:** sglang[#47802](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47802) (GQA group > 16 not divisible by 16 reading the wrong KV head) and sglang[#48397](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48397) (Triton's AMD buffer ops cap addressing at 2 GiB) both hit the paths we run on gfx942/gfx950.
- **gfx1250 work lives on branches, not main:** atom[#2874](https://github.com/ROCm/ATOM/pull/2874) (fp8 KV + unified attention, +45.7% output tok/s on MI450) and aiter[#7468](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7468) both merged into `alizaidy/gfx1250-qwen-support`; gfx1250 GDN KKT + inverse went 138.8 → 64.6 µs in atom[#3009](https://github.com/ROCm/ATOM/pull/3009).
- **tokenspeed is a pure port source this week:** 8.88× on greedy-row top-k (tokenspeed[#2779](https://github.com/lightseekorg/tokenspeed/pull/2779)) and a 50%-error FP8 KV probability-shift fix (tokenspeed[#2897](https://github.com/lightseekorg/tokenspeed/pull/2897)) both map onto AITER kernels.
