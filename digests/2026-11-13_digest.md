## Signals across repos

### ROCm

- **The gfx950 FP8 MLA verify family is silently wrong, and the fixes are all post-pin** —
  wrong rows with correct-looking output on qseqlen1/2/4 and bf16 QH128
  ([aiter#7617](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7617), [aiter#7622](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7622),
  [aiter#7623](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7623), [aiter#7631](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/issues/7631),
  [aiter#7349](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7349)); [#7617](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7617)
  lands four days after the tag vLLM pins.
- **int32 offset overflow is still the most common silent-corruption class** —
  [sglang#47029](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47029) (KV slots past 2.1M tokens),
  [vllm#65926](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65926) (DSv4.1 K-cache gather),
  [vllm#63049](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/63049) (W4A16 at 2^31), [tokenspeed#3247](https://github.com/lightseekorg/tokenspeed/pull/3247) (gather rows 4 GiB early).
- **"Works until the buffer is big" is a recurring ROCm failure mode** — 4 GiB expert weights
  ([aiter#7586](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7586)), 2–4 GiB KV per layer
  ([vllm#62606](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/62606)), 4 GiB KV view on CDNA3
  ([vllm#67446](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/67446)), 2^32-byte bf16 KV pool
  ([ATOM#3120](https://github.com/ROCm/ATOM/issues/3120)).
- **ROCm 10.1 + clang 24 regresses FP8 prefill 7–15×** until the `-mllvm` workaround ships
  ([aiter#7099](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/issues/7099), [aiter#7103](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7103)) —
  a toolchain pin decision, not a kernel decision.
- **Deterministic mode became usable on gfx950, then broke again** — AITER decode/prefill are now
  admitted under determinism ([sglang#44489](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/44489),
  [sglang#45601](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45601)), but AITER GEMM/MoE still make
  results batch-dependent ([sglang#43788](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43788)) and batch-variant
  `mhc_pre` runs at 0.07× throughput ([sglang#49812](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/49812)).

### Quantization

- **fp8 KV cache is the week's worst silent-wrong area** — Kimi-K3 GSM8K 0.975 → 0.305
  ([vllm#64106](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/64106)), a 2^-13 → 2^-22 scale floor change
  that silently coarsens DeepSeek-V4.1 KV ([sglang#48500](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48500)),
  checkpoint scales applied to a *bf16* cache ([sglang#48747](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/48747),
  fix [sglang#48748](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48748)), half-size scales on gfx942
  compressed-tensors ([vllm#66586](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/66586)).
- **MXFP4/MXFP8 shapes fail silently at the K tail** — CK-Tile stage1 with `model_dim = 256 mod 512`
  ([aiter#7136](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7136)), ragged-K e8m0 ([aiter#7047](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7047)),
  A8W4 intermediate padding ([sglang#49278](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/49278),
  [vllm#61816](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/61816)).
- **INT4 QuickReduce clipped group scales above 480** — the mechanism behind MiniMax-M3 MMMU
  0.66 → 0.27: fixed in [aiter#7258](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7258) (in v0.1.29), with a
  vLLM escape hatch ([vllm#64929](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64929)) and a CDNA3
  routing fix ([vllm#67446](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/67446)).
- **Quant wins that need no kernel work:** fp8 indexer cache on gfx942 (+32% KV capacity,
  [vllm#62270](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62270)), MXFP8 linear on tuned FlyDSL GEMM
  ([vllm#65479](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65479)), MXFP4 KV on gfx942
  (1.5–1.7× more tokens per GB, [vllm#65899](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65899)).

### Online quantization

- **vLLM's rotation path landed** — block Hadamard input rotation wired into online `mxfp4_hadamard32`
  and Quark online-rotation checkpoints ([vllm#62750](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62750)).
- **e4m3fnuz vs e4m3 remains the sharpest ROCm quant edge** — ModelOpt FP8 MoE expert weights now
  converted with the fnuz factor ([vllm#66580](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/66580)), KV scales
  likewise ([vllm#66586](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/66586)), and a real bug where
  `fp8_e5m2` was advertised while the writer stored e4m3 ([vllm#66090](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/66090)).
- **Requant cost is startup time we pay per recipe** — gfx942 MXFP4→int4 for Kimi-K3 under pure TP:
  ~19 min → 25 s ([vllm#64004](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64004)), TP8 total 22.5 → 3.5 min,
  converted weights byte-identical.
- **swiglu_limit was accepted and silently ignored on FP8 paths** — gpt-oss/GLM `swiglu_limit=7.0`
  MoE on gfx942/gfx950 ([aiter#7361](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7361), merged 2026-11-16, just after the window).

### Elsewhere

- **CUDA-only MegaMoE shows the gap** — fused-shared-expert FP8 MegaMoE gives GLM-5.3-Flash DEP4
  1.6× the output throughput of DEP4 generic ([vllm#60895](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60895));
  the ROCm GLM-5.3 lane has no equivalent, and ATOM's answer is a route-selection change
  ([ATOM#3187](https://github.com/ROCm/ATOM/pull/3187)).
- **gfx1250 is being brought up under flags, not ports** — MLA-absorb in BF16
  ([ATOM#2489](https://github.com/ROCm/ATOM/pull/2489)), a per-arch AITER sha in sglang's Dockerfile.
- **NVIDIA exactness work is upstream of us** — DSv4 `topk_v2` exact/deterministic modes with an
  exact default on ROCm ([sglang#47960](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47960)).
- **tokenspeed is the portable-kernel source, not a target** — its gfx950 MoE/attention/sampling
  kernels describe mechanisms ([tokenspeed#2779](https://github.com/lightseekorg/tokenspeed/pull/2779),
  [tokenspeed#3121](https://github.com/lightseekorg/tokenspeed/pull/3121),
  [tokenspeed#2962](https://github.com/lightseekorg/tokenspeed/pull/2962)) that AITER and vLLM can reuse.

## ROCm/aiter

Two themes: **the gfx950 FP8/bf16 MLA decode family is silently wrong** (five separate LDS and
split-merge bugs, four of them merged after v0.1.29 was cut), and **ROCm 10.1 + clang 24 regresses
eight-wave FP8 GEMMs 7–15×** until the `-mllvm` workaround is in the image. Perf work in the window
was mostly MoE sorting and dispatch-table tuning. vLLM pins aiter `v0.1.29` (cut 2026-11-09), so
rows marked *post-pin* do not reach us until the next bump.

## sgl-project/sglang

sglang spent the week making **deterministic mode actually deterministic** on ROCm: AITER decode and
prefill were admitted under determinism (they partition KV into fixed tiles), a fixed-order
two-stage all-reduce replaced a batch-variant one, and the residual batch-dependence in AITER
GEMM/MoE is now documented with reproducers. The correctness tail is long — five separate
silent-wrong paths on `ROCM_AITER_*` that vLLM shares.
